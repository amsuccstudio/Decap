ABOUT FAWLUX

Crafted for the Confident.

FAWLUX is a Nigerian bespoke menswear brand dedicated to craftsmanship, individuality, and timeless style. The brand combines the richness of African fashion with contemporary sophistication to create refined clothing for the modern man.

With a focus on fit, comfort, and attention to detail, FAWLUX aims to create garments that reflect personal style and inspire confidence.

About the Founder

Fawaz Abdulkareem

Founder of FAWLUX

Fawaz Abdulkareem is a Nigerian fashion designer and entrepreneur with a passion for tailoring, creativity, and craftsmanship. He founded FAWLUX with a vision to build a distinctive menswear brand rooted in quality, elegance, and individuality.

Driven by a commitment to developing his craft, Fawaz seeks to create clothing that allows men to express their personal style with confidence. His journey reflects a dedication to continuous growth and the ambition to build a lasting name in the fashion industry.

The Story Behind the Name

The name FAWLUX originated from Fawaz Luxury. “FAW” is drawn from Fawaz, while “LUX” is inspired by luxury.

The name reflects the vision behind the brand: to create clothing that embodies elegance, quality, and distinction. More than a name, FAWLUX represents an ambition to build a brand recognised for its craftsmanship, refined style, and attention to detail.

What We Do

FAWLUX specialises in bespoke menswear, including Agbada, Kaftan, and other tailored garments designed around individual style and fit. Every garment is approached with care and precision, with attention to the details that contribute to its fit, comfort, and finishing. The aim is to bring each client's vision to life through clothing that balances African heritage with contemporary style.
